Privacy & Tracking

Barkey Pet Sitting respects your privacy and uses limited information to operate this website, respond to inquiries, and understand how visitors use the site.

Information You Provide

You may choose to share information through the contact form or scheduling process, including your name, email address, phone number, pet-care details, scheduling information, and anything else you include in a message.

Barkey Pet Sitting uses this information to respond to inquiries, arrange services, communicate with clients, and provide pet-sitting services. Contact and agreement forms are submitted through Formspree, while scheduling information entered on Cal.com is handled by Cal.com.

Website Analytics

This website uses Google Analytics 4 to understand general website usage and improve the visitor experience. Analytics information may include pages visited, approximate visit duration, browser or device type, referring website or traffic source, general geographic area, and interactions with website features.

Barkey Pet Sitting reviews this information as general usage data and does not use it to personally identify individual website visitors.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

Google Analytics may use cookies or similar browser technologies to collect website usage information. Whether these technologies work can depend on your browser settings, privacy tools, and device.

Third-Party Services

The website currently connects with these services:

  • Google Analytics helps measure website visits and feature interactions.
  • Formspree receives and delivers information submitted through the contact and pet-sitting agreement forms.
  • Cal.com provides external scheduling pages for meet-and-greets and pet-care services.
  • Google Fonts supplies the website’s typefaces from Google’s servers.
  • Venmo provides an external payment option linked from the payment pages.

When you use or load these services, the provider may process information according to its own privacy practices.

Your Choices

You can adjust or block cookies in your browser settings, use private browsing, or turn on other browser privacy controls. You can also install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Disabling cookies may affect how some website features work, but you can still contact Barkey Pet Sitting by using the information on the contact page.
